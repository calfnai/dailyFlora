HOW TO USE

A quiet guide to keeping a flower on the screen.

Open today’s flower
01

Fullscreen

Use the fullscreen button for an uninterrupted room-like view.

Open fullscreen tutorial

These keys work throughout the daily bouquet view.

← →Move to the previous or next day
↑ ↓Move closer to or farther from the bouquet
+ −Move closer to or farther from the bouquet
ROpen a random date
0Restore the default view
PChange the camera route preset
SpacePause or resume automatic rotation
HHide or reveal the interface
FEnter or leave fullscreen
OOpen or close VIEW
?Open this guide again
EscClose the guide, then leave fullscreen
02

Hand control

Enable hand control from VIEW, then use an open hand to move through the bouquet.

Open hand-control tutorial

Enable hand control from VIEW, allow camera access, then use an open hand to move through the bouquet. The camera is used locally by the browser.

Either hand ☝️Cycle bouquet density
Either hand ✌️Cycle render detail
Either hand, three fingersToggle clock mode
Either hand 👍Resume or stop the automatic camera
Either hand, four fingersRequest fullscreen; confirm with the on-screen button
Either hand ✊Safety brake: stop all controls
Right thumb + indexPinch when close; hold and move on X / Y
Either hand fully openPush or pull depth
Either hand slightly closedMove the palm to rotate the camera
Both hands visibleSpread acceleration assists zoom

Camera and MediaPipe privacy details

03

Clock mode

Automatic clock mode begins after two quiet minutes.

Open clock tutorial

Clock mode shows the current time, date, a rotating quote, and a clock-focused bouquet layout. Open it from VIEW, or let Auto start it after the selected quiet interval. Press Esc to exit.

One bouquet a day. No need to possess it.
