DailyFlora guide

HOW TO USE

DailyFlora is made for quiet looking. Open VIEW when you want more control; leave it closed when you simply want the bouquet to remain.

Choose a day

Use the calendar to open a date, return to today, or select a random day. The left and right arrow keys move one day at a time.

Shape the view

Zoom in or out, restore the default framing, change bouquet density and rendering precision, or adjust the automatic camera route.

Fullscreen controls

Press Esc to leave fullscreen, R for a random date, + / − to zoom, 0 to reset, Space to pause, H to hide the interface, and ? for help.

Gesture control

Camera permission is requested only after you actively enable hand control. MediaPipe processes the live image in the browser; DailyFlora does not upload, save or transmit camera frames.
