EVERY
DAY

01

A bouquet for every day

Out of the dark, a bouquet slowly takes shape.

It does not ask what you are doing.
It does not ask to be watched.

It simply arrives.

Every day carries its own colour.

Some open brightly,
like a morning greeting.

Some lean quietly,
like the air after rain.

Some remain almost white,
as though nothing needs to be said.

They emerge from the depth of black.

Light rests upon the petals.
A trace of wind remains between the leaves.

24H
02

A window within the day

This is not an image that needs to be finished.

It is a window opened onto one page of the calendar.

When you pass by, it is there.
When you turn away, it remains.

On Mac, the bouquet can continue to move gently with the passing hours.

Time no longer sits apart from the flowers.
It flows quietly through them.

03

Keep a day in bloom

Some flowers ask to be seen again.

So you keep them.

Your personal garden is not made for display.
It grows from the moments that made you pause.

One bouquet belongs to one date.
One date carries one atmosphere.

When you return, something of that day is still there.

01The colour.

02The stillness.

03The temperature
of the moment.

04

Grow one of your own

You may also create a bouquet for a day, a person, or a memory that has not yet found its name.

Begin with

An image. A feeling. A sentence.

Let them become the seed.

The bouquet grows from there, taking on its own form, rhythm and character.

You can keep it, shape it, return to it and share it with someone else.

05

A daily digital bloom

Made to live quietly
within everyday space.

DailyFlora is a digital art experience made to live quietly within everyday space.

Not as another object asking for attention.

As a bouquet that remains.

A small presence beside your books, your music, your work and the things you have chosen to keep close.

Every day brings a new flower.
Some days, however,
you may want to leave in bloom.
