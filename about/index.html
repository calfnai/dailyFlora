About DailyFlora

A BOUQUET FOR EVERY DAY

Where dates meet flowers. Out of the dark, a bouquet slowly takes shape. It does not ask what you are doing. It simply arrives.

See today’s bouquetHow to use

01 · What it is

A daily digital bloom.

Every day carries its own colour.

Some open brightly, like a morning greeting. Some lean quietly, like the air after rain. Some remain almost white, as though nothing needs to be said.

They emerge from the depth of black. Light rests upon the petals. A trace of wind remains between the leaves.

DailyFlora is a digital art experience made to live quietly within everyday space—not as another object asking for attention, but as a bouquet that remains.

02 · Why one every day

A window within the day.

01

One date, one atmosphere

This is not an image that needs to be finished. It is a window opened onto one page of the calendar. When you pass by, it is there. When you turn away, it remains.

02

Keep a day in bloom

Some flowers ask to be seen again. Your personal garden grows from moments that made you pause. When you return, something of that day is still there: the colour, the stillness, the temperature of the moment.

03

Grow one of your own

Begin with an image, a feeling or a sentence. Let them become the seed. The bouquet grows from there, taking on its own form, rhythm and character.

04

Directed, not automated

Generation rules, botanical structure, composition, colour and movement are reviewed as an aesthetic system. Technology makes variations possible; CALFN LAU determines the product direction and final visual judgement.
