### About

# A BOUQUET FOR EVERY DAY

*Where dates meet flowers.*

DailyFlora brings a daily digital bouquet to your screen. Look around it, explore the details, or let it turn while you read, work or listen to music.

Read the story ↓

**Today · A bouquet in motion**

### 01 · A day, in flowers

A date becomes a bouquet.

Petals catch the light. Stems reach into the dark. Each day brings a small change of scene.

**Every day carries its own fragrance, its own thoughts.**

The brightness of a morning. The scent of rain. A pale flower that brings someone to mind.

DailyFlora is a way to look at the passing days through flowers, colour and movement.

### 02 · Flowers on your screen

*every 24H*

**Make room for flowers.**

Open today's bouquet in your browser, or choose another date to explore. Turn it, move closer, or fill your screen with flowers.

Switch on Clock Mode to see the time beside the bouquet.

For a desktop display, explore the macOS and Windows betas on the Platforms page.

### 03 · Keep a day in bloom

**A bouquet you want to see again.**

Save it to My garden.

A date might bring back a birthday, a meeting, or an ordinary afternoon. A bouquet might simply have a colour you love.

Collect the ones you want to return to. Your garden takes shape with each bouquet you keep.

*A colour you loved. A day you remember. Someone you thought of.*

### 04 · Begin with a reference

**Start with an image that means something to you.**

Choose it for its colours, its mood, or the shape of a branch. Add a note about what you would like to keep or change.

In the Beta garden, you can submit that image and your preferences as a bouquet-generation request. Follow its progress in My garden and open the result when it is ready.

### 05 · An eye for flowers

**Colour, form, and room to breathe.**

A branch reaching beyond the petals. A cluster of colour. Space between the stems.

DailyFlora explores how these details work together in a digital bouquet. Look around it: the outlines overlap, the gaps open, and a different arrangement comes into view.

*Come back for another day. Stay a little longer with a bouquet you love.*

## 中文

### 关于

# 每一天，都有一束花

*日期与花，在此相遇。*

DailyFlora 让每天的数字花束出现在你的屏幕上。你可以转动视角，看看花间的细节，也可以在阅读、工作或听音乐时，让它缓缓转动。

阅读故事 ↓

**今天 · 一束动态的花**

### 01 · 把一天，看成一束花

一个日期，化作一束花。

花瓣接住光，枝条伸入暗处。每天，屏幕上的风景有一点不同。

**每一天都有自己的香气与思绪。**

清晨的明亮，雨后的气味，或是一朵让你想起某个人的浅色花。

透过花、色彩和转动的光影，看看日子如何一天天经过。

### 02 · 屏幕上的花

*every 24H*

**给花留一点位置。**

在浏览器里打开今日花束，也可以选一个日期，看看那一天的花。转一转，拉近一些，或者让花铺满屏幕。

打开时钟模式，让时间显示在花束旁。

如果想把它留在桌面上，可以到 Platforms 查看 macOS 和 Windows 的 Beta 版本。

### 03 · 让一天继续盛放

**有些花，你想再看一次。**

把它收藏到「我的花园」。

一个日期可能让你想起生日、一次见面，或某个平常的下午。也可能没有特别的理由，只是喜欢这束花的颜色。

留下那些想再看的花束。你的花园，就这样慢慢有了自己的样子。

*喜欢的一种颜色。记得的某一天。想起的一个人。*

### 04 · 从一张参考图开始

**选一张对你有意义的图片。**

你可能喜欢它的配色、气氛，或一根枝条的形状。再写几句话，说说想保留什么、改变什么。

在 Beta 花园中，你可以提交图片和偏好，发起一次花束生成请求。在「我的花园」查看进度，完成后打开生成的花束。

### 05 · 看花的细节

**色彩、形态，还有花间的空隙。**

伸出花瓣之外的枝条，聚在一起的颜色，茎叶之间留出的空间。

DailyFlora 尝试让这些细节组成一束数字花。换个角度，轮廓相互交叠，空隙渐渐展开，眼前的构图也随之变化。

*改天再来看看。遇到喜欢的一束，就多停留一会儿。*
